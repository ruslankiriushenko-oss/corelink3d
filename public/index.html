About CoreLink 3D Studio AI

CoreLink 3D Studio AI is an advanced browser-based 3D workspace powered by Three.js (WebGL 2.0) and integrated local AI LLMs (Qwen 2.5 Coder 7B via Ollama & vLLM). It enables creators to inspect, edit, and manipulate 3D models and lighting environments using real-time natural language commands.

Key Features:

  • AI Natural Language Control: Control PBR materials, shaders, camera views, and lighting setups via an interactive AI assistant interpreter.
  • Advanced Material & Shader Editor: Real-time adjustments for Metallic, Roughness, Normal, and Emissive properties, including custom Sci-Fi Overlay and Wireframe modes.
  • Lighting & Environment Presets: Customizable studio, sunset, cyberpunk, and night lighting setups with live HDRI map support.
  • 3D Inspector & Analytics: Detailed mesh node hierarchy inspection, draw call analytics, poly/vert count metrics, and GLTF/GLB binary scene export.
