About CoreLink 3D

CoreLink 3D is a modern, high-performance browser-based 3D workspace and scene editor powered by Babylon.js (WebGPU native with WebGL2 fallback).

Monolithic 3D Workspace: Clean dark graphite environment with interactive gizmos (Q/W/E/R) and hierarchical scene outliner.
PBR Principled Material Editor: Full real-time control over Roughness, Metallic, Normal, Emissive, and Alpha properties.
Lighting & Environment: Adjustable scene color, light intensity, and shadows.
Full Scene Compatibility: Binary glTF (.glb), JSON glTF (.gltf), and custom project bundle (.cl3d) import/export.
